Questions

Straight answers.

Is a real dermatologist reviewing my case?

Yes. Dr. Amit Om, MD, a board-certified dermatologist and Mohs surgeon, reviews every case and writes every prescription himself. You can look up his board certification with the American Board of Dermatology.

How does it work without a video visit?

You answer a few questions and send photos of your skin. Dr. Om reviews them and replies in the app with your plan. No video call, no phone call, no waiting room.

How fast will I hear back?

Usually within 1 to 2 business days of a complete submission. If a photo isn't clear enough or Dr. Om needs more information, he'll ask before writing your plan.

Who can use UNZIT?

Adults 18 and older. We're starting in North Carolina, so right now you need to be physically in North Carolina, with more states coming. You'll verify your identity with a photo ID and a quick selfie before your first visit.

How much does it cost?

Care is $39 a month: dermatologist review, a written plan, monthly check-ins and messaging, with prescriptions sent to the pharmacy you choose. All-In is $89 a month: everything in Care, plus prescription medication shipped to your door and weekly check-ins for your first 8 weeks. The price you see is the price you pay.

Do you take insurance?

UNZIT doesn't bill insurance, and the monthly plan isn't covered by insurance. On Care, you can still use your insurance at your pharmacy for your medication. All-In includes medications on the UNZIT formulary, so no insurance is needed.

What will I be prescribed?

Only what's right for your skin, and only if you need a prescription. Options include tretinoin and other topical retinoids, benzoyl peroxide, clindamycin, azelaic acid, doxycycline, spironolactone for hormonal acne in women, and hydroquinone for dark marks.

What do you treat?

Acne on the face, jawline, chest, back and shoulders, including adult and hormonal acne, plus the dark marks breakouts leave behind. Severe, cystic or scarring acne may need in-person care, and Dr. Om will tell you if so. We don't evaluate moles or other spots; anything new or changing should be seen in person.

How long until I see results?

Most acne treatments take 8 to 12 weeks of steady use, and some people break out a little more in the first few weeks before it gets better. We'll check in along the way. We won't promise clear skin by a date.

How do I cancel?

Online, any time, with no phone call. Cancelling stops the next renewal, and you keep care through the end of the month you've paid for. If Dr. Om decides at your first review that UNZIT isn't right for you, you get a full refund.

Can I use UNZIT if I'm pregnant or breastfeeding?

Yes, just tell us. Some acne medicines aren't safe in pregnancy, so your plan will only use options that are.
